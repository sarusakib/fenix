## Brand palette
- Navy: #0B1736
- Teal: #008080 / #14B8A6
- Gold: #FFD700 / #E7B800

Use the same symbol and wordmark proportions across all product surfaces. Do not introduce unrelated AI, globe, cart, or stock iconography.
